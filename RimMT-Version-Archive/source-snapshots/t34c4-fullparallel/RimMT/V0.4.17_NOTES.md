# RimMT V0.4.17 Playtest — Parallel Work Prefilter

Target: RimWorld 1.5.4063 rev1072.

## Why this version exists

V0.4.16.3 proved that aggressive worker-built Reachability profiles can be consumed at scale: the target save produced more than 874k profile hits and roughly 792k authoritative profile results with 82,106 sampled parity checks. Only one sampled mismatch occurred (`predTrue/liveFalse`), with zero `predFalse/liveTrue` false-negative reachability samples. Normal `Reachability.CanReach` samples became very cheap, while the remaining visible JobGiver spikes continued to concentrate in `GenClosest` plus cell-scanning WorkGivers such as GrowerSow and BuildRoof.

V0.4.17 therefore begins moving Work candidate rejection onto spare cores.

## New feature: `parallel.workPrefilter`

The initial production whitelist is deliberately exact:

- Vanilla `WorkGiver_GrowerSow`
- Vanilla `WorkGiver_GrowerHarvest`
- Vanilla `WorkGiver_BuildRoof`

The current Vanilla scan is never delayed or reordered. RimMT wraps the exact `PotentialWorkCellsGlobal` result, yields every cell back to Vanilla unchanged, and records those same `IntVec3` cells while Vanilla consumes them. When enumeration finishes, the captured cells are split into worker batches. The published result can only prove a narrow set of hard negatives for a later scan.

### Worker-side read-only hard negatives

GrowerSow may reject a cell when the worker observes one of Vanilla's early hard-fail conditions:

- no desired plant is configured for that cell;
- the cell is outside the current growth season;
- the desired plant is already present in the cell.

GrowerHarvest may reject a cell when:

- there is no plant;
- the plant is not currently harvestable / mature / able to yield;
- the plant is manual-harvest-only and the current request is not forced.

BuildRoof may reject a cell when:

- it is no longer in the Build Roof area;
- it is already roofed.

RimMT intentionally does **not** call roof support flood-fill (`RoofCollapseUtility.WithinRangeOfRoofHolder` / `ConnectedToRoofHolder`) on workers because Vanilla's `Map.floodFiller` is shared scratch state.

## Safety / bounded-risk policy

This version intentionally allows a narrow whitelist of live Verse reads on worker threads. It still does not allow worker-side Job creation, reservations, Pawn/Thing/Map mutation, WorkGiver priority changes, or final target commit.

- Unknown or positive worker results always run Vanilla.
- The first 16 negative predictions for each map/kind are shadow-validated against Vanilla.
- After warmup, one negative in 32 is sampled against Vanilla.
- A sampled false negative clears that kind's snapshot and cools it for 300 main-thread frames.
- Eight sampled false negatives globally fuse `parallel.workPrefilter` OFF and restore full Vanilla scanning.
- Worker live-read exceptions are contained per cell and become `unknown`; they do not escape the worker batch.
- Snapshot authority is short-lived (45 main-thread frames).
- A foreign Harmony owner on the exact authoritative `HasJobOnCell` / `JobOnCell` method disables only that WorkGiver kind.

## Reachability retained

V0.4.16 aggressive Reachability remains enabled with its existing warmup, 1/16 parity sampling, per-profile cooldown and global fuse. The full runtime Harmony census remains available. The one observed V0.4.16.3 mismatch was `predTrue/liveFalse`; no `predFalse/liveTrue` sample was observed. V0.4.17 does not add a per-CanReach diagnostic wrapper because that would add overhead to more than a million calls merely to diagnose an extremely rare sample.

## What to check in runtime reports

Look for `Parallel Work prefilter V0.4.17` and record:

- `kindCompat(sow/harvest/roof)`
- `sourceCellsCaptured`
- `buildsScheduled / buildsPublished / buildsRejected / buildsStale`
- `workerBatches / workerCells / workerReadFaults`
- `negativeHits / authoritativeFalse`
- `fastNegatives(sow/harvest/roof)`
- `shadowSamples / shadowMatches / parityMismatches`
- `avgWorkerCellUs / avgWorkerBatchUs / maxWorkerBatchUs`

The main performance acceptance target is lower JobGiver long-tail cost without increasing frame-time variance. Compare `JobGiver_Work.TryIssueJobPackage` average/P95/max and the bounded slow traces, especially GrowerSow, GrowerHarvest and BuildRoof.
