DBH Animal Water Source Fix - RimWorld 1.5

Purpose
- Prevent animal drinking job loops when Dubs Bad Hygiene gives an animal an inventory water Ingest job while a usable basin/trough is also available.

Behavior
- Patches only DubsBadHygiene.JobGiver_DrinkWater.TryGiveJob(Pawn).
- Only runs for animals.
- Only intervenes when DBH has already selected an inventory item with WaterExt.SeekForThirst as an Ingest target.
- Calls DBH's own ClosestSanitation.FindBestDrink using the same urgency and search-range rules as DBH.
- If DBH returns a basin/sanitation fixture, replaces the inventory Ingest job with DBHDrinkFromBasin.
- If DBH finds no usable basin, leaves the original inventory water job unchanged.
- On reflection/API failure, fails open and leaves DBH behavior unchanged.

Performance
- No global Pawn scans.
- No GameComponent/WorldComponent tick loop.
- No Pawn_JobTracker patch.
- No extra work unless an animal is thirsty and DBH has selected carried water.
