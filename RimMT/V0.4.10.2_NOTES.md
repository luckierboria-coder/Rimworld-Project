# RimMT V0.4.10.2 playtest

V0.4.10.2 is a conservative follow-up to the validated V0.4.10/V0.4.10.1 JobGiver candidate partition.

## Goal

Reduce residual JobGiver -> GenClosest -> Reachability.CanReach spikes in the common medium candidate range observed after removing Loadout Compositing from the test stack.

## Change

- Existing V0.4.10.1 stable 16-cell ring partition remains unchanged.
- Supported candidate arrays with 96-320 entries may receive a second stable 8-cell ring refinement.
- The refinement is skipped for tightly clustered sets (fine max ring < 4 or fewer than 16 candidates at fine ring >= 2).
- Candidate membership is never changed.
- Reachability, WorkGiver validators, reservations, priorities and final Jobs remain Vanilla/main-thread authoritative.
- Medium-set refinement is main-thread only; no worker dispatch is used for this small arithmetic pass.
