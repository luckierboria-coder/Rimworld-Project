VOE Caravan Needs Recovery - V3.1 (RimWorld 1.5)

V3.1 fix:
- Fixed the recovery Harmony hook for RimWorld 1.5. V3 incorrectly targeted Pawn_NeedsTracker.NeedsTrackerTick; RimWorld 1.5 uses NeedsTrackerTickInterval(int delta).
- Recovery now uses the game's actual delta directly: gain = 0.166 / 2500 * delta.
- This removes the separate per-Pawn elapsed-tick bookkeeping and makes the 16.6 percentage-point-per-hour rate deterministic with world ticking.

Behavior:
- A caravan stopped on any VOE-compatible Outpost_* tile recovers every Pawn Need.
- Every Pawn Need recovers by 16.6 percentage points per in-game hour.
- Recovery is percentage-based, so Needs with MaxLevel other than 1.0 recover at the same relative rate.
- Recovery is capped at 100%.
- Normal caravan need satisfaction is suppressed while recovery is active to avoid double recovery and inventory food/drug consumption.
- Starting to move or leaving the Outpost tile immediately restores normal Need ticking.

Compatibility:
- Vanilla Outposts Expanded.
- VOE: Additional Outposts and other Outpost_* extensions.
- Normal caravans.
- Vehicle Framework vehicle caravans through Vehicle Framework's Caravan compatibility layer, including boarded passengers.

Not changed:
- Health/Hediff progression is not modified.
- Non-Need systems such as psyfocus are not modified.
