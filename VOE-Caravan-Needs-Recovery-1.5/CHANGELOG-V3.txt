VOE Caravan Needs Recovery - V3.2 (RimWorld 1.5)

V3.2 fix:
- Corrected the V3 growth implementation after verifying the actual RimWorld 1.5 API: Pawn_NeedsTracker uses NeedsTrackerTick(), matching the already-successful V2 freeze hook.
- Pawn_NeedsTracker.NeedsTrackerTick now only suppresses normal NeedInterval decay while recovery is active.
- Actual recovery is performed from Caravan_NeedsTracker.NeedsTrackerTick, the caravan-level need entry point called from Caravan.Tick.
- Recovery is batched at most once per 150 ticks per caravan. This avoids per-Pawn recovery clocks and avoids traversing every Need every game tick.
- Outpost tile detection is cached for 250 ticks. Movement state is never cached, so recovery stops immediately when the caravan resumes travel.

Behavior:
- A caravan stopped on any VOE-compatible Outpost_* tile recovers every Pawn Need.
- Every Pawn Need recovers by 16.6 percentage points per in-game hour.
- Recovery is percentage-based, so Needs with MaxLevel other than 1.0 recover at the same relative rate.
- Recovery is capped at 100%.
- Vanilla caravan auto-satisfaction is suppressed while recovery is active, so food/drugs are not consumed by that handler during the stay.
- Starting to move or leaving the Outpost tile restores normal Need ticking.

Compatibility:
- Vanilla Outposts Expanded.
- VOE: Additional Outposts and other Outpost_* extensions.
- Normal caravans.
- Vehicle Framework vehicle caravans. VehicleCaravan inherits Caravan and calls base.Tick(), and Vehicle Framework exposes boarded passengers through the caravan compatibility layer.

Performance:
- Map-spawned Pawns exit the Pawn patch immediately before any world/caravan lookup.
- World-object scanning is cached by tile for 250 ticks.
- Need recovery loops run only once per caravan per >=150 ticks, not every tick and not from every Pawn.

Not changed:
- Health/Hediff progression is not modified.
- Non-Need systems such as psyfocus are not modified.
