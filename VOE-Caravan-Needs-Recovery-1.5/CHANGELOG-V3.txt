VOE Caravan Needs Recovery - V3 (RimWorld 1.5)

New behavior:
- A caravan stopped on any VOE-compatible Outpost_* tile no longer merely freezes Pawn Needs.
- Every Pawn Need recovers by 16.6 percentage points per in-game hour (0.166 / 2500 ticks).
- Recovery is percentage-based, so Needs with MaxLevel other than 1.0 recover at the same relative rate.
- Recovery is capped at 100%.
- Normal caravan need satisfaction is suppressed while recovery is active to avoid double recovery and inventory food/drug consumption.
- Leaving the recovery state immediately restores normal Need ticking.

Compatibility:
- Vanilla Outposts Expanded.
- VOE: Additional Outposts and other Outpost_* extensions.
- Normal caravans.
- Vehicle Framework vehicle caravans through Vehicle Framework's Caravan compatibility layer, including boarded passengers exposed through CaravanUtility/GetCaravan.

Not changed:
- Health/Hediff progression is not modified.
- Non-Need systems such as psyfocus are not modified.
